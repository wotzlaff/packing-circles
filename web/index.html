04 / THE MATHEMATICS

A closer look at
the problem.

Simple shapes. A surprisingly intricate search.

01

A lower bound

Area alone gives the bound R ≥ √(Σ rᵢ²). Circles leave gaps, so a valid packing generally needs more room than this.

02

Searching for a fit

The objective combines radius with quadratic penalties for constraint violations. Penalty weights change during the search, so the objective is not the minimum feasible radius.

03

Reading the geometry

Contacts mark circles close to tangency. Raw trial geometry can overlap or cross the boundary; violations are shown in red.
